Match the build to your stage

Spending too much too early is the most common startup technology mistake. Start with what tests your idea.

  1. Test the idea

    A focused landing page that explains the offer and collects interest.

  2. Build the MVP

    The smallest web application that delivers the core value to early users.

  3. Grow carefully

    Add features based on real usage, with maintenance and monitoring in place.
