Every month

  1. Install updates. Operating system, browser, and the apps you use most. Restart when asked.
  2. Check your backup works. Do not just confirm it ran. Open it and restore one file to make sure you can get your data back.
  3. Free up storage. Aim to keep at least 15 to 20 percent of the drive free. Clear Downloads and empty the Trash or Recycle Bin.
  4. Restart fully. A real restart clears memory and finishes background updates.
  5. Tidy your browser. Remove extensions you do not use and close the tabs you have kept open for weeks.

Every few months

  • Review start-up apps and turn off the ones you do not need. See speeding up Windows start-up.
  • Clean the outside. Shut down, unplug, and wipe the keyboard and screen with a slightly damp microfiber cloth. Never spray liquid directly onto the device.
  • Check the vents are not blocked by dust. A fan that runs constantly is a sign the computer is getting too hot.
  • Check battery health on laptops. A battery that drains quickly or swells needs attention. Stop using a swollen battery and book a repair.

Once a year

  • Review which accounts and apps you still use, and remove the rest.
  • Make sure important accounts use strong, unique passwords and two-step sign-in.
  • Decide whether an upgrade would extend the computer's life.
