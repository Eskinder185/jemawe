1. Do a hard refresh

A hard refresh reloads the page without using saved copies of its files.

  • Windows (Chrome, Edge, Firefox): Ctrl + Shift + R, or Ctrl + F5
  • Mac (Chrome, Firefox): Cmd + Shift + R
  • Safari: Option + Cmd + R

2. Try a private window

Open a private or incognito window and visit the site. If it works there, the problem is your stored data or an extension, not the website.

3. Clear cache and cookies

  • Chrome, Edge, Firefox: press Ctrl + Shift + Delete on Windows, or Cmd + Shift + Delete on a Mac, choose a time range, and clear cached files and cookies.
  • Safari: open Safari > Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data, search for the site, and remove it.
{{icon:info}}

Clearing cookies signs you out of websites. Make sure you know your passwords, or use a password manager, before you do it.

4. Turn off extensions

Ad blockers and privacy extensions sometimes break website features. Disable them one at a time, reload, and see which one causes the problem.

5. Update your browser

Outdated browsers can fail on modern websites. Check for updates in your browser's settings or About page and restart it.
