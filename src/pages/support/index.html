Before you contact support

Two minutes here can make the session much faster.

  • {{icon:check}}Restart the device once. It fixes more than people expect.
  • {{icon:check}}Note the exact error message, or take a screenshot.
  • {{icon:check}}Know your operating system and roughly when the problem started.
  • {{icon:lock}}Never send passwords to anyone, including us.
