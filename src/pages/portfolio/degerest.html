Overview

Degerest Ethiopian Restaurant pairs a warm hospitality landing page with a fast, interactive digital menu. Guests can explore 86 dishes and drinks across 14 categories, toggle between English and Amharic (አማርኛ), and jump directly to coffee, ordering, or reservations.

The opportunity

Large traditional menus can feel overwhelming on a phone screen. Guests need an easy way to filter by dietary preference (such as vegan samplers or vegetarian dishes), sort by price, search for specific dishes, and read clear descriptions without pinching and zooming.

Key features

  • Bilingual English and Amharic support (including dual-language category and dish headings).
  • Searchable and sortable menu covering 86 dishes and drinks across 14 categories.
  • Interactive filters for Appetizers, Signature Dishes, Shareable Platters, Vegan Samplers, Kids Menu, Coffee & Hot Drinks, and more.
  • Print-friendly menu view, dish favorites, meal plan tray, and dedicated navigation for About, Coffee, Order Here, and Reservations.
