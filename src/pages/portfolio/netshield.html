What it is

NetShield presents an approach to home internet troubleshooting, device awareness, and suspicious-link guidance. The public site explains issues in everyday language and offers an interactive demo.

The problem it addresses

People often know that a connection feels slow or a device looks unfamiliar without knowing what to check next. The preview organizes an issue into what happened, what to do, and how to verify a fix.

What the public preview shows

  • A landing-page diagnosis illustration and a path into the interactive demo.
  • A demo dashboard with sample internet, device, protection, and home-sensor cards.
  • Guided next steps for a website-loading problem and unfamiliar devices.

Availability and tradeoff

The demo uses made-up sample data. It does not measure the visitor's network. The public site states that account registration and live sensors require its local backend. This makes the interaction reviewable without presenting simulated results as real diagnostics.

The implementation stack is not published in the accessible project materials, so no framework or backend technology is attributed here.
