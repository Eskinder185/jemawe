Weekly

  • Confirm the latest backup completed.
  • Check the site loads and the home page looks right on a phone.
  • Review form submissions and make sure they are arriving.

Monthly

  • Apply software, framework, and plugin updates, then check key pages.
  • Send a test message through every form.
  • Check for broken links and missing images.
  • Confirm hours, prices, staff, and contact details are still correct.

Quarterly

  • Restore a backup to a test location to prove it works.
  • Run a speed check and optimize any large new images.
  • Review who has login access, and remove people who no longer need it.
  • Check the site with a keyboard only: can you reach every link and button?

Yearly

  • Confirm the domain renewal date and that auto-renew is on.
  • Check the security certificate (the padlock) renews automatically.
  • Review hosting costs and whether the plan still fits.
  • Read the site as a new customer would, and update outdated pages.
