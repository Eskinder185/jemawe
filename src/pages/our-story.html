2024

Two friends start building together

Jemawe begins as a collaboration between two friends working on technology projects together, combining web development with hands-on computer support.

Projects

Real projects for real communities

The team builds a community website for the Ethiopian Community Association in Atlanta, the Roadmap to Role career-development app, and the Tewahedo Daily prayer and calendar companion. See the work.

Now

Becoming a technology services company

Jemawe is being introduced publicly as a professional company with three divisions: digital solutions, IT services, and computer services. The founder is establishing project management, financial administration, and customer support.

Next

Client portal and support plans

Planned, not yet launched: a secure client portal for projects, support tickets, and repairs, and published IT support plans.
