How a project moves from idea to launch

The same four stages apply whether you need a five-page website or a custom application. You approve each stage before the next begins.

  1. Consultation

    We learn what you need, who it is for, and what success looks like.

  2. Planning

    A written scope, sitemap or feature list, timeline, and quote for your approval.

  3. Development

    We design, build, and test, sharing progress so you can give feedback early.

  4. Launch and support

    We launch together, hand over what you need, and stay available for support.

Common questions

Short answers to what people ask before getting in touch.

How much does a website cost?{{icon:chevron-down}}

It depends on the number of pages, features, and content you need. After a short consultation we send a written quote, so you know the cost before anything starts.

Do you offer support 24 hours a day?{{icon:chevron-down}}

No. Remote IT support is scheduled. When you submit a request, we confirm a session time with you directly.

Will you ask for my computer password?{{icon:chevron-down}}

No. Our forms never ask for passwords, and a remote session starts only when you approve it on your own screen.

Can you work on a website someone else built?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Often, yes. We review the site first and tell you plainly whether maintaining, improving, or rebuilding it makes the most sense.
