Who we are

Jemawe is a technology solutions company. We work across three divisions: digital solutions, IT services, and computer services, so a small business, school, restaurant, or nonprofit can bring most of its technology questions to one team.

We are early in our journey as a company, and we would rather be clear about that than pretend otherwise. You will not find invented client counts or awards here. You will find real projects, a careful process, and people who explain things plainly.

Mission and vision

Our mission

To empower businesses, organizations, and individuals through reliable, innovative, and accessible technology solutions.

Our vision

To become a trusted technology partner that helps businesses grow, modernize, and succeed through practical digital innovation.

Core values

These shape how we quote, build, and support. If we fall short of one, we want to hear about it.

{{icon:shield}}Reliability

Do what we said, when we said, and tell you early if that changes.

{{icon:eye}}Transparency

Written scope, separate parts and labor, and honest answers about limits.

{{icon:accessibility}}Accessibility

Technology that works for as many people as possible, including our own explanations.

{{icon:lightbulb}}Practical innovation

Modern tools when they help, simple ones when they are enough.

{{icon:users}}Customer focus

Design around the people who will use the website, tool, or device every day.

{{icon:lock}}Respect for data

Ask for the minimum, never collect passwords, and protect what we hold.

Our approach

Every engagement follows the same shape, whether it is a website, a support session, or a repair.

  1. Understand first

    We ask questions before recommending anything.

  2. Agree in writing

    Scope, cost, and timeline you approve.

  3. Do the work carefully

    Tested, documented, and explained.

  4. Stay available

    Support and maintenance after the handover.
