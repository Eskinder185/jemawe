What needs more than a remote session

We are clear about limits. Remote support will not fix a computer that cannot start, a failing drive, liquid or drop damage, or a startup disk that needs a full operating system reinstall from external media. Those cases go through our computer repair process with a diagnosis first.

We also will not bypass passwords, activation locks, or security features on a device you cannot prove you own.
