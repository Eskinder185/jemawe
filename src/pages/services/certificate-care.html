Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about TLS certificates, renewal automation, and website security.

Does my hosting provider already renew my certificate?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Many hosting platforms include automated renewal attempts, but renewals can fail without warning when DNS records change, challenge tokens expire, firewalls block validation traffic, or server software updates alter configuration files. Furthermore, renewal does not always mean deployment: a new certificate may be generated in cloud storage while the active web server continues serving an expiring one. Certificate care verifies both the renewal and the active deployment.

Why would I need certificate care if certificates are free?{{icon:chevron-down}}

While public certificate authorities like Let’s Encrypt provide certificates at no software charge, the certificates do not configure, test, or troubleshoot themselves. You are paying for technical expertise: properly configuring DNS and server bindings, ensuring all subdomains are covered, testing renewal mechanisms, verifying deployment, monitoring endpoints proactively, and resolving failures before they cause site outages.

What is the CA/Browser Forum schedule for certificate lifetimes?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Under CA/Browser Forum Ballot SC-081 (officially adopted into the TLS Baseline Requirements), maximum certificate lifespans are being reduced in scheduled stages: dropping to 200 days in March 2026, 100 days in March 2027, and 47 days in March 2029 (with domain validation data reuse dropping to 10 days). Because certificates must soon be renewed every few weeks, manual calendar tracking is no longer practical, making renewal automation and verification essential.

Do I need to move my website to AWS?{{icon:chevron-down}}

No. We support multiple providers and hosting environments, including Cloudflare, AWS, standard Linux servers, and conventional web hosts. We evaluate your current hosting setup and implement certificate management tools that fit your existing infrastructure.

Can you review a website built by another developer?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Yes. We regularly review websites, DNS configurations, and hosting setups created by other teams or agencies. We start with an assessment of your public endpoints, DNS records, and hosting controls, then provide a written summary and proposed scope.

Does HTTPS make my whole website secure?{{icon:chevron-down}}

No. TLS certificates protect data in transit between a visitor’s browser and your server, ensuring that transmitted data is encrypted and authenticated. TLS does not protect against vulnerabilities in your website code, weak administrative passwords, unpatched server operating systems, or compromised databases. A complete security posture requires application updates, access controls, regular backups, and ongoing maintenance.

What information should I include in my inquiry?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Please share your primary public website address, any subdomains or API endpoints you need covered, your current hosting or DNS providers (if known), and any recent certificate issues or upcoming project deadlines. Please do not send passwords, API tokens, private keys, or sensitive internal credentials.
