What we repair

{{icon:laptop}}

Laptop repairs

The most common laptop problems are batteries, storage, charging, and overheating.

  • {{icon:check}}Battery that drains fast, swells, or no longer charges
  • {{icon:check}}Slow or failing hard drives replaced with SSDs
  • {{icon:check}}Charging port and power adapter problems
  • {{icon:check}}Overheating, fan noise, and internal dust build-up
  • {{icon:check}}Keyboard and trackpad issues, where parts are available
{{icon:monitor}}

Desktop repairs

Desktops are easier to open and upgrade, which often makes repair the cheaper option.

  • {{icon:check}}Computers that will not power on or randomly shut down
  • {{icon:check}}Power supply replacement
  • {{icon:check}}Storage, memory, and component replacement
  • {{icon:check}}Cleaning, cable management, and cooling checks
{{icon:stethoscope}}

Diagnostics

Every repair starts by confirming the cause, so you do not pay for parts that were not the problem.

  • {{icon:check}}Hardware tests for storage, memory, battery health, and temperatures
  • {{icon:check}}Software checks to rule out a problem a repair would not fix
  • {{icon:check}}A plain-language summary of what we found and your options
{{icon:cpu}}

Components and compatibility

Not every part fits every machine. We check compatibility before ordering anything.

  • {{icon:check}}Model-specific part matching for memory, storage, batteries, and power supplies
  • {{icon:check}}Honest advice when parts are discontinued or cost more than the device is worth
  • {{icon:check}}Operating system reinstallation after storage replacement, with your data plan agreed first

The repair workflow

You approve the quote before any repair work begins. If the repair is not worth it, you can stop after the diagnosis.

  1. Request a repair

    Tell us the device, model, and what is happening.

  2. Diagnosis

    We confirm the cause and check part availability and compatibility.

  3. Quote approval

    A written quote listing parts and labor separately.

  4. Repair

    The approved work, nothing more without asking you first.

  5. Testing and return

    Tested again, then returned or ready for pickup.

Before you hand over a device

  • {{icon:database}}Back up anything important if the computer still works. Repairs rarely affect data, but storage problems can.
  • {{icon:lock}}Do not write your password on the device or the form. If we need to sign in to test, we will arrange it with you directly.
  • {{icon:file}}Note the exact model, usually on a label underneath or in the system settings.
