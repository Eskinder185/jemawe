The guest's experience, step by step

  1. Scan the QR code

    A small code on the table or window opens the menu in the phone's browser. No app to install.

  2. Open the menu

    The page loads quickly even on a weak signal, showing your name and today's menu.

  3. Browse categories

    Guests jump between starters, mains, and drinks instead of scrolling one long list.

  4. View dishes

    Clear names, short descriptions, prices, and dietary tags, readable without zooming.

Your QR code does not change when the menu does

The printed code points to your menu page. Update prices or swap a dish, and every code in the restaurant shows the new version straight away.
