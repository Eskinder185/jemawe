How a support request works

Five steps from your first message to a confirmed fix.

  1. Submit a request

    Use the support form to tell us your device, operating system, and what is going wrong.

  2. Explain the problem

    We may ask a few follow-up questions, or for a screenshot of the error.

  3. Schedule a session

    We agree a time that suits you. Sessions are scheduled, not round the clock.

  4. Receive assistance

    You start the remote session, watch every step, and can end it at any time.

  5. Confirm resolution

    We check the problem is gone and explain what was changed.

Privacy and session authorization

Giving someone access to your computer requires trust. These rules apply to every remote session.

  • {{icon:lock}}You start every session. We send a session code; nothing connects until you approve it on your own screen.
  • {{icon:eye}}You can watch and stop at any time. Closing the session ends our access immediately.
  • {{icon:shield}}No unattended access. We do not install tools that let us connect later without you.
  • {{icon:close}}We never ask for your passwords through forms, email, or chat. If a step needs one, you type it yourself.
  • {{icon:file}}We only open what the problem requires and tell you before looking at anything else.
  • {{icon:headset}}Reputable remote support software is used for every session. {{todo "Name of the remote support tool Jemawe uses"}}

When remote support is not the right fit

Some problems need hands on the hardware. If the computer will not power on, has physical damage, or a failing part, remote support cannot fix it. In those cases we will tell you so and suggest a repair booking instead of charging for a session that cannot help.

Remote support also requires the computer to start and connect to the internet.
