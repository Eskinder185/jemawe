Where we help small businesses

{{icon:monitor}}

Workstation and device setup

New computers set up correctly once, so they are ready on day one.

  • {{icon:check}}Operating system updates, user accounts, and security settings
  • {{icon:check}}Email, office software, and the business apps your team uses
  • {{icon:check}}Printers, shared drives, and Wi-Fi connections
  • {{icon:check}}Backup configured and tested before the device is handed over
{{icon:users}}

Employee computer onboarding

A repeatable checklist for every new starter, and a clean handover when someone leaves.

  • {{icon:check}}A written setup checklist specific to your business
  • {{icon:check}}Accounts created with the access each role needs, and no more
  • {{icon:check}}Offboarding steps so former staff lose access promptly
{{icon:refresh}}

Preventive maintenance

Regular check-ups that catch problems before they interrupt work.

  • {{icon:check}}Update, storage, and backup checks on each computer
  • {{icon:check}}Software configuration reviews and clean-up
  • {{icon:check}}Connectivity troubleshooting for the office network and printers
{{icon:checklist}}

Device inventory support

Know what equipment you have, who uses it, and when it needs replacing.

  • {{icon:check}}A simple record of devices, owners, and purchase dates
  • {{icon:check}}Warranty and replacement reminders
  • {{icon:check}}Upgrade recommendations instead of early replacement where it makes sense

One-time help or recurring support

Some teams need help once, when computers are set up. Others want a regular check-in and someone to call.

We can work on individual requests as they come up, or agree a recurring arrangement with a defined scope, schedule, and response window. Recurring plans are described on the support plans page.
