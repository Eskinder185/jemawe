Our website development process

Six stages, each with something for you to review. Nothing moves forward without your approval.

  1. Discovery call

    Goals, audience, examples you like, and what the site must do.

  2. Scope and quote

    A written page list, feature list, timeline, and price for approval.

  3. Content and structure

    Sitemap and page outlines; we help shape copy you supply.

  1. Design

    Home and key page designs on real content, revised with your feedback.

  2. Build and test

    Development, then testing on phones, tablets, desktops, and keyboards.

  3. Launch and handover

    Go live on your domain, with instructions for editing and next steps.

Website questions

How long does a website take?{{icon:chevron-down}}

It depends on the number of pages and how ready your content is. The written scope includes a timeline for your specific project before you commit.

Can I edit the website myself?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Yes, when it makes sense. We can add a content editor for the pages you update often, such as events or menus, and show you how to use it.

Do you write the content?{{icon:chevron-down}}

You know your business best, so you supply the core information. We help structure and edit it for the web, and can agree additional writing in the scope.

Who hosts the website?{{icon:chevron-down}}

We recommend a hosting option that suits your site and budget during planning. Hosting and domain fees are paid to the provider and listed separately from our work.
