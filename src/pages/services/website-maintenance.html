What maintenance covers

{{icon:refresh}}

Updates

Framework, plugin, and dependency updates applied and checked.

{{icon:database}}

Backups

Regular backups of site files and content, with a tested restore process.

{{icon:pulse}}

Monitoring

Uptime and broken-link checks so problems are spotted early.

{{icon:file}}

Content changes

Text edits, new images, team updates, events, and new pages.

{{icon:wrench}}

Bug fixes

Things that stop working on a browser or device, investigated and fixed.

Performance checks

Periodic speed reviews, image optimization, and cleanup.

Security maintenance

Security patches, form spam protection, and certificate checks.

Support arrangement

A known way to send requests and a stated response window.

Defined limits, stated up front

We do not offer unlimited support. Every maintenance arrangement spells out what you get, so there are no surprises on either side.

Each arrangement states in writing

  • {{icon:check}}Which tasks are included, and how often they happen
  • {{icon:check}}How much change work is included each month
  • {{icon:check}}How to send requests and the expected response window
  • {{icon:check}}How work beyond the included amount is quoted and approved
  • {{icon:check}}Notice period to change or end the arrangement

Usually quoted separately

  • {{icon:info}}New features, new sections, or a redesign
  • {{icon:info}}Hosting, domain, and third-party subscription fees
  • {{icon:info}}Writing large amounts of new content

How a maintenance request works

  1. Send the request

    Describe the change or problem, with a link and screenshot if you have one.

  2. We confirm scope

    We confirm it is covered, or send a quote first if it is not.

  3. We make the change

    Changes are tested before they go live on your site.

  4. You review

    We let you know it is done and what changed.
