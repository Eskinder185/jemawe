Website development

What kinds of websites do you build?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Business websites, landing pages, redesigns, restaurant websites with digital menus, and websites for schools and community organizations. See website development.

Can I update the website myself after launch?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Yes, where it makes sense. We can add an editor for the content you change often, such as events, menus, or team members, and show you how to use it.

Will my website work on phones?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Yes. We design for phones first and test layouts from small phones to wide desktop screens.

Do I own my domain and hosting?{{icon:chevron-down}}

We recommend that your domain and hosting accounts are registered in your organization's name, so you stay in control whoever maintains the site.

Maintenance

What does website maintenance include?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Updates, backups, monitoring, content changes, bug fixes, performance and security checks. Each arrangement lists exactly what is included. See website maintenance.

Is maintenance unlimited?{{icon:chevron-down}}

No. Every arrangement has a defined amount of work and a stated response window. Anything beyond it is quoted first.

Can you maintain a site someone else built?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Often. We review it first and tell you plainly whether maintaining, improving, or rebuilding makes the most sense.

IT support

No. Support is scheduled during business hours. When you submit a request, we confirm a session time with you.

Which systems do you support?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Windows and macOS computers, common business software, browsers, email, printers, and Wi-Fi connections.

Remote sessions

How does a remote session start?{{icon:chevron-down}}

We give you a session code, and you approve the connection on your own screen. Nothing connects without your action.

Can you connect to my computer when I am not there?{{icon:chevron-down}}

No. We do not use unattended access. Every session needs you present to approve it, and you can end it at any time.

Will you ask for my password?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Never. Our forms do not ask for passwords, and if a step needs one during a session, you type it yourself.

Hardware repairs

How much will my repair cost?{{icon:chevron-down}}

That depends on the diagnosis and part availability. You get a quote with parts and labor listed separately, and nothing is repaired until you approve it.

Can every laptop be upgraded?{{icon:chevron-down}}

No. Many thin laptops and recent Macs have memory and storage soldered to the board. We check your model before recommending anything.

Will I lose my files during a repair?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Most repairs do not affect data, but storage problems can. Back up if you can, and tell us in the booking form whether your data is backed up so we can plan safely.

Data handling

What do you do with the information I send?{{icon:chevron-down}}

We use it to respond to your request and provide the service. We do not sell it. See the privacy policy.

Do you look at my files during support or repair?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Only what the problem requires, and we tell you before opening anything else.

Do you store payment card details?{{icon:chevron-down}}

No. Payments will be handled through an established payment provider; we never store card numbers ourselves.
