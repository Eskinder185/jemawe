Draft policy

This policy needs review for Jemawe's actual operations and jurisdiction before forms are enabled.

Who we are

This website is presented by Jemawe. Its legal business identity and privacy contact details have not been published.

Information you give us

When you use a form on this website, such as contact, quote, consultation, support, or repair booking, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, organization, and details about your project, device, or problem. We ask only for what we need to respond. Our forms never ask for passwords or payment details.

Current status of our forms

While the forms are marked as unavailable, information entered into them is checked in your browser and is not sent to Jemawe. Form handling and its providers will be described here before submissions are enabled.

How we use information

  • To reply to your request and provide the service you ask for.
  • To keep records of work we have agreed with you.

We do not sell personal information.

Remote support sessions

During a remote session we only view what the problem requires, and we tell you before opening anything else. Sessions start only with your approval and end when you close them.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The website forms do not save your answers in browser storage.

Service providers

When we connect hosting, email, form, scheduling, or payment providers, we will list them here and describe what they process. Payments will be handled by an established payment provider; we will never store card numbers ourselves.

How long we keep information

Retention periods for future enquiries and service records must be finalized before the forms are enabled.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we must keep by law. Use the contact page to make a request.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and show the date of the latest update.
