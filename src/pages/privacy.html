Information you give us

When you use a form on this website, such as contact, quote, consultation, support, or repair booking, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, organization, and details about your project, device, or problem. We ask only for what we need to respond. Our forms never ask for passwords or payment details.

Current status of our forms

At the time of writing, the forms on this website are not connected to any service. Information typed into them is checked in your browser and is not sent or stored. This section will be updated when a form service is connected, including which provider processes submissions.

How we use information

  • To reply to your request and provide the service you ask for.
  • To keep records of work we have agreed with you.

We do not sell personal information.

Remote support sessions

During a remote session we only view what the problem requires, and we tell you before opening anything else. Sessions start only with your approval and end when you close them.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies or store information in your browser, and it does not use analytics or advertising trackers. {{todo "Update this section if analytics, embedded maps, or other third-party services are added"}}
